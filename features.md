MicroCampus: Comprehensive Feature List
Audience: Marketing & Sales Team
Document Version: 1.0.3
Date: November 6, 2025
Overview: This document provides a comprehensive breakdown of the MicroCampus platform, detailing all modules and functionalities. Use these features and examples to create marketing materials, website content, and sales scripts.
1. Central Administration & School Management
This is the command center for the entire school. It gives administrators complete control over all academic and non-academic operations.
Unified Statistics Dashboard
Function: A single-screen overview of all vital school statistics.
Example: At a glance, the Principal sees "92% Average Daily Attendance," "৳8,50,000 Fees Collected this Month," and "52 New Admission Enquiries" in real-time.
Comprehensive User Management
Function: Create, edit, and manage profiles for all admins, teachers, students, and staff.
Example: An admin searches for "Md. Karim" (Teacher), views his profile, updates his assigned class from '7A' to '7B', and resets his password.
Role & Permission Management
Function: Grant specific permissions to different user roles to ensure data security.
Example: Admin can create a 'Librarian' role that only has access to the 'Library Management' module, but cannot view 'Fees & Accounting'.
School & Academic Year Settings
Function: Customize the platform with the school's brand and manage academic sessions.
Example: Admin uploads the new school logo, sets the system name to "Sunrise International School," and defines the "2026-2027" academic session.
Database & System Integrity
Function: Secure the school's data with backup and restore tools.
Example: Admin schedules an automatic full database backup every night at 2 AM and can restore from a previous point in case of data loss.
2. Academic & Curriculum Management
Tools to build, manage, and schedule the school's entire academic structure.
Class & Section Management
Function: Define all classes and their respective sections (e.g., shifts, versions).
Example: Admin creates "Class 6" and adds three sections: 'A (Morning, Bangla)', 'B (Day, English)', and 'C (Morning, English)'.
Subject & Syllabus Management
Function: List all subjects and assign them to specific classes.
Example: Admin assigns "Higher Math" and "Biology" to 'Class 9' and 'Class 10', but not to 'Class 8'.
Teacher & Subject Assignment
Function: Map teachers to the specific classes and subjects they will teach.
Example: Admin assigns 'Mrs. Salma' to teach 'English' for 'Class 7A' and 'Class 7B'.
Class Routine (Weekly Timetable)
Function: A visual tool to build the weekly class schedule for all sections.
Example: Admin builds the 'Class 8A' routine, assigning 'Math' to the 1st period on Sunday. The system auto-flags a conflict if the assigned teacher is already busy at that time.
Academic Calendar
Function: A school-wide calendar for all important dates.
Example: Admin posts an event for "Annual Sports Day" on Feb 15th, which then appears on all parent, student, and teacher dashboards.
3. Student Information System (SIS)
A complete digital record for every student, from admission to graduation.
Student Admission & Profile
Function: Manually enroll new students and maintain their detailed digital profiles.
Example: An admin enrolls "Fatima Ahmed," inputs her photo, guardian's contact, address, and past academic info, instantly generating a unique Student ID 'S-2026-045'.
Student Promotion
Function: Promote students to the next class at the end of an academic year.
Example: After final results, the admin selects all "Passed" students from 'Class 8' and moves them to 'Class 9' in one bulk action.
Transfer Certificate (TC) Generator
Function: Instantly create and print official Transfer Certificates.
Example: A parent requests a TC. The admin clicks 'Generate TC' for that student, and a print-ready PDF is immediately created with the school's official letterhead.
Exam Admit Card Generator
Function: Automatically generate admit cards for exams.
Example: Before finals, the admin generates admit cards for all 'Class 10' students, complete with their photo, exam schedule.
4. Parent & Guardian Engagement Portal
A dedicated login for parents to stay connected with the school and their child's progress.
Student Progress Dashboard
Function: View their child's academic results, attendance, and school notices.
Example: A parent logs in and sees their child has a '95%' attendance rate, an 'A' in Math, and a 'B-' in English for the mid-term.
Integrated Online Fee Payment
Function: View fee invoices and pay them securely online.
Example: A parent receives an SMS reminder, logs into the portal, sees "৳2,000 Tuition Fee Due," and pays it instantly using bKash, Nagad, or SSLCommerz.
Communication & Notifications
Function: Receive official announcements and access teacher information.
Example: A parent views the "Parent-Teacher Meeting" announcement and finds their child's science teacher's contact details to schedule a call.
5. Teacher & Faculty Dashboard
The teacher's digital assistant for all classroom and academic tasks.
Attendance & Mark Management
Function: A simple interface for teachers to manage daily attendance and exam marks.
Example: During the first period, a teacher takes attendance for 'Class 7A' on their mobile. After an exam, they enter the 'Biology' marks for all students.
Auto-Grading System
Function: The system automatically calculates grades (e.g., A+, A, B) and GPA based on the marks entered.
Example: The teacher enters "85" for a student, and the system automatically converts it to "A+" and "5.00" grade points.
Class Routine & Resources
Function: View personal timetables and access student/parent contact information.
Example: A teacher logs in to check their personal routine for Tuesday and sees they have 'Class 8B' at 11:00 AM.
Result Sheet Submission
Function: Finalize and submit all marks for administrative approval.
Example: After entering all marks for the final exam, the teacher clicks 'Submit Result Sheet', locking the marks and sending them to the admin for publishing.
6. Smart Attendance System
A robust system for tracking both student and staff attendance.
Daily Attendance Tracking
Function: Record daily attendance for students and staff.
Example: A teacher takes student attendance by 9:00 AM. Separately, staff members sign in at the front office, and their attendance is logged.
Automated Absentee Notifications
Function: Automatically send SMS alerts to parents if their child is marked absent.
Example: A student is marked 'Absent'. An automated SMS is sent to their parent: "Dear Parent, your child, Rahim, is absent today (Nov 6)."
Attendance Reporting
Function: Generate detailed attendance reports by class, date, or for an individual student.
Example: The Vice-Principal generates a report for 'October' and sees 'Class 9A' has the lowest average attendance at 88%.
Hardware Integration Support
Function: Compatibility with external attendance devices.
Example: The school can connect its existing RFID or biometric fingerprint scanner to automatically push attendance data into the MicroCampus software.
7. Finance & Fee Management Module
A complete accounting solution for managing school revenue and expenses.
Flexible Fee Structure Setup
Function: Create and customize all types of fees the school collects.
Example: Admin defines fee types: 'Monthly Tuition (৳1500)', 'Annual Sports Fee (৳1000)', and 'Transport Fee - Mirpur Route (৳800)'.
Individual & Bulk Fee Collection
Function: Collect fees, track due lists, and send reminders.
Example: The accountant generates monthly invoices for all students in one click. They can then view a "Due List" showing which students have not paid.
Online Payment Gateway (Bangladeshi)
Function: Natively integrates with popular local payment gateways.
Example: A parent paying online can choose from bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or any card/MFS supported by SSLCommerz.
Printable Receipts
Function: Generate digital or print receipts for every transaction.
Example: When a parent pays in cash at the counter, the accountant records the payment and prints a 2-copy receipt instantly.
Expense Management
Function: Log and categorize all school expenses.
Example: The admin records an expense for "Utility Bill (৳15,000)" and another for "Staff Salaries (৳1,20,000)".
Financial Reports
Function: Generate key financial summaries.
Example: At the end of the year, the admin generates a "Profit & Loss Summary" showing total income from fees vs. total expenses.
8. Examination & Result Management
An end-to-end system for creating exams, entering marks, and publishing results.
Exam Creation & Schedule
Function: Create exam terms (e.g., Mid-Term, Final) and publish their schedules.
Example: Admin creates the "Half-Yearly Exam 2026" and sets the date for the 'Mathematics' exam, which then appears on parent/student portals.
Flexible Marks Entry
Function: Teachers can enter marks manually or upload them in bulk.
Example: A teacher with 50 students downloads the CSV template, fills in the marks in Excel, and uploads the single file to update all marks instantly.
Online Result Publishing
Function: Publish final results to the student and parent portals with a single click.
Example: On result day, the admin clicks "Publish Results for Class 10," making the digital report cards visible to all Class 10 parents.
Printable Marksheets & Report Cards
Function: Automatically generate detailed, professional report cards.
Example: A parent downloads their child's "Final Report Card," a PDF showing marks, grades, GPA, and teacher remarks for every subject.
Class Ranking System
Function: Automatically generate class and subject-wise student rankings.
Example: The admin generates a "Top 10 Students" list for 'Class 5' based on their final exam GPA.
9. Integrated Communication Hub
Tools to keep the entire school community informed and connected.
Multi-Channel Alerts (SMS & Email)
Function: Broadcast messages to specific groups via SMS or email.
Example: Admin types an announcement: "School will be closed tomorrow due to unavoidable circumstances." and broadcasts it as an SMS to all 'Parent' and 'Teacher' groups.
Digital Noticeboard & Circulars
Function: A central place for all official announcements.
Example: An "Inter-School Debate Competition" circular is posted, which appears on the login dashboard for all users.
10. Digital Library System
Manage the school library's catalog and circulation.
Book Catalog & Search
Function: Digitize the library's book collection with search functionality.
Example: A student searches the portal for "Gitanjali" by 'Tagore' and sees that 2 out of 3 copies are currently available in the library.
Issue, Return & Fine Tracking
Function: Track which books are borrowed, their due dates, and any fines.
Example: The librarian issues a book to a student. The system automatically calculates a "৳10 per day" fine if it's returned 5 days late.
11. Transport & Route Management
Organize and manage the school's transportation services.
Vehicle, Driver & Route Management
Function: Map all vehicles, drivers, and their designated routes.
Example: Admin defines "Route 1 (Dhanmondi)," assigns "Bus 5" and "Driver Kamal" to it, and sets the monthly fee for that route.
Student Transport Assignment
Function: Assign students to a specific transport route.
Example: "Student Fatima" is assigned to "Route 1." The "Transport Fee (৳800)" is then automatically added to her monthly school fee invoice.
Transport Attendance
Function: Optional attendance tracking for transport users.
Example: A bus monitor takes attendance on the bus to ensure all assigned students have boarded safely.
12. HR & Payroll Management
Manage all teaching and non-teaching staff, from attendance to payroll.
Staff Profiles & Attendance
Function: Maintain detailed profiles and track attendance for all staff.
Example: The HR manager views a teacher's profile to see their attendance record, joining date, and contact information.
Leave Management System
Function: A digital system for leave requests and approvals.
Example: A teacher submits a "Sick Leave" request through their portal. The admin receives a notification, approves it, and the leave is automatically deducted from the teacher's quota.
Automated Salary Generation
Function: Generate monthly salaries with custom allowances and deductions.
Example: At the end of the month, the admin generates salaries, applying deductions for 'Absent Days' and adding the 'House Rent' allowance.
Payslip Generation
Function: Automatically create and email detailed payslips to staff.
Example: After running payroll, the system automatically emails a PDF payslip to every teacher, detailing their earnings and deductions for the month.
13. Advanced Reporting & Analytics Dashboard
Powerful, data-driven insights for better school management.
Attendance Analytics
Function: Deep dive into attendance patterns.
Example 1 (Summary): Generate "October Attendance – Class 7A Avg: 89%."
Example 2 (Actionable): Run an "Absence Report" to find "Students with >5 absences this month" for counseling.
Financial Analytics
Function: Track revenue and financial health.
Example 1 (Collection): View "Std Fee Collection Report" showing "Class 9: 30 paid out of 43 students in October."
Example 2 (Overview): Generate the "Annual Finance Summary 2025" to see a high-level Profit & Loss statement.
Academic Performance Analytics
Function: Compare and analyze exam results to find trends.
Example 1 (Term-over-Term): "Class 9A: Term 1 GPA 3.82 → Term 2 GPA 4.11 (+0.29)."
Example 2 (Comparative): "Class 7A Avg 75% vs Class 7B Avg 78% (Science)."
Example 3 (Individual): "Show 'Student 'A': +5% over class average' to highlight high-achievers."
Example 4 (Demographic): "Generate 'Gender-based Performance': 'Class 10: Boys Avg 3.89, Girls Avg 4.01'."
Example 5 (Subject): "Create a 'Subject Difficulty Trend' bar graph showing 'ICT' has the lowest average score, indicating a need for teacher training."
Data Export
Function: Export any report or data table.
Example: Admin exports the "Full Student List" to Excel or a "Final Marksheet" as a PDF for printing.
14. System Settings & Customization
Multi-Language Support (not available, by default ‘English’)
Function: Switch the entire interface between languages.
Example: An admin can use the portal in "English," while a parent can switch their view to "Bangla."
Theme & Brand Customization
Function: Match the platform's look and feel to the school's brand.
Example: Admin uploads the school's logo and changes the portal's color scheme from the default blue to the school's brand color (e.g., green).

Online Admission Portal
Function: A public-facing portal for new student applications.
Example: New parents can fill out a customizable admission form online, upload documents, pay the application fee, and track their application status.
Hostel (Dormitory) Management
Function: Manage all aspects of the school's residential facilities.
Example: Admin will be able to manage room/bed allocation, track hostel-specific fees, and log student attendance and visitors within the hostel.
